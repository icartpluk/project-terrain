# Robotic system requirements (conceptual)

## Mobility and navigation
- Operate in GPS-denied, low-light simulated tunnels.
- Maintain uncertainty-aware local maps and obstacle detection.
- Detect loss of localization and transition to a safe stop.

## Manipulation
- Evaluate tool interfaces for generic debris clearing and handling in inert testbeds.
- Measure grip reliability, stability, reach, and payload margins.
- Use purpose-built equipment rather than assuming humanoid hands can perform every task.

## Perception and operator control
- Provide remote cameras, environmental sensing, and clear operator feedback.
- Indicate sensor health and uncertainty; do not claim identification of nuclear materials from ordinary camera imagery.
- Support supervised teleoperation and explicitly authorized autonomy.

## System integration
- Use modular interfaces and testable boundaries between robot, tools, and safety controller.
- Benchmark against non-humanoid alternatives.
- Require specialist review for any hazardous-environment adaptations.

## Success criteria
Set quantitative performance thresholds only after testbed and risk requirements have been reviewed.
