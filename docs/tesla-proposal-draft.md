# Draft proposal for Tesla Optimus team
**Working draft — not sent**

**Subject: Project TERRAIN — exploring humanoid robotics for hazardous underground recovery**

Dear Tesla Optimus Team,

I am proposing Project TERRAIN (Teleoperated Exploration, Recovery Robotics, and Autonomous Infrastructure Navigation), a research concept to evaluate whether advanced humanoid robotics could help reduce human exposure during authorized hazardous-material recovery in damaged underground facilities.

A motivating example is the challenge of recovering hazardous nuclear materials from damaged underground sites in Iran. The proposal does not presume access to any site or claim that Optimus is currently qualified for this task. Instead, it asks whether a controlled research program could establish the feasibility of remotely supervised robotic navigation, mapping, generic debris handling and integration with specialist safety systems.

I propose beginning with a nonhazardous simulation and physical mock-up. Tesla Optimus could be assessed alongside tracked, wheeled and other purpose-built remote platforms. Independent safety controls, secure authorized commands, loss-of-communications behavior, fault-injection tests and human supervisory authority would be central requirements.

Beyond terrestrial remediation, independently validated capabilities in navigation, manipulation and infrastructure preparation could inform future robotic operations on Mars.

I would welcome an opportunity to share the concept and discuss whether it aligns with Tesla's robotics research priorities.

Sincerely,
